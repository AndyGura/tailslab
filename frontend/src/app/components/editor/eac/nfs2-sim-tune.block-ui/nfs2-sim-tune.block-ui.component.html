Flight

The step a car leaves the ground its roll rate (about its forward axis) is multiplied by the initial jump roll attenuate and its pitch rate (about its right axis) by the initial jump road pitch damp. Human cars then also get roll -= half their lateral acceleration × jump roll due to car turn, and pitch += half their forward acceleration × jump pitch due to acc and brake. In the air the car falls with 1 + extra flight gravity times the gravity. Ground max speed change is stored but not read by the game: the newton limits the ground speed change with half the horizontal speed, at least 16 m/s, instead.

The telepole launch value is the crash threshold: a crash whose strength (6 × the speed into a wall, the impulses of car hits and landings) is above it counts: the landing reaction, the yaw thrown into roll and pitch near the ground and the wreck timer; a smackable object hit that hard is launched. AI racers in arcade style wait 3 times the ticks, a car tumbling off a tumble wall 256 ticks. A car whose |roll rate| + |pitch rate| is below the stop tumbling speed on the ground loses a quarter of them each step. The reset puts the car the reset forward slices past the crash slice (backward in a backward race), the reset altitude above the road (half on slices with a ceiling), moving at the reset launch speed along it; for the invincibility ticks it meets no other car.

The deflection angular range applies to cars that are not human hitting a slice wall: when the cosine of the angle between the wall's direction and the car's road forward is above it (the angle below the range in degrees) the car turns toward the wall's direction, else it is steered along it. The speed loss applies to every car sliding along a wall.
